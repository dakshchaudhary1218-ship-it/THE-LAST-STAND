# THE LAST STAND

a zombie survival game i made for hack club phantom.

## story

a guy is living a normal life with his girlfriend. then a zombie attack happens and she dies. zombies start spreading everywhere and he becomes the "last man stand". now he has to fight them with guns and try to cure the disease.
